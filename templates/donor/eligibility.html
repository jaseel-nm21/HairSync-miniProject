Hair Donation Best Practices
Clean & 100% Dry
Wash and thoroughly dry hair before cutting. Damp hair can develop mildew in storage and cannot be crafted into wigs.
Secure with Elastic Bands
Tie hair tightly in a ponytail or braid at both ends before cutting to preserve hair cuticle direction.
Length Matters
Most medical wigs require at least 20 cm (8 inches) of hair length, pulled straight from tip to cut.
Layered & Gray Hair
Gray hair is warmly welcomed! Layered hair is accepted as long as the longest layer meets minimum requirements.
