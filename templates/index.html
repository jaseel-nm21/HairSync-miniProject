Our Mission

Empowering Cancer & Alopecia Warriors

Hair loss caused by chemotherapy, radiation, or chronic autoimmune conditions like Alopecia can profoundly impact a patient's self-esteem and mental well-being during their hardest battle.

HairSync replaces scattered spreadsheets and informal social media calls with a centralized, secure digital platform — connecting willing donors directly with verified NGOs who craft life-changing wigs for those in need.
